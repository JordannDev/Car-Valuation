# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vehicle buyers comparing a selected year, make, and model, optionally refining the search with mileage. The interface is used on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Make vehicle selection straightforward and present available valuation information clearly. Catalog data, valuations, and comparable listings come from the API.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, controlled. Keep the existing light theme, familiar typography, and blue action accents described in DESIGN.md.

## Design Principles

- Keep the required vehicle input before optional mileage.
- Give the initial search room to breathe, then make space for results.
- Use motion to explain state changes and skeletons to preserve context during loading.
- Distinguish insufficient listing evidence from failed requests, and historical estimates from current or projected prices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard selection, visible focus, readable labels, loading announcements, and reduced-motion alternatives. Keep the form usable on small screens and at increased text sizes.
